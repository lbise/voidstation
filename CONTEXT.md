# Voidstation

Voidstation is a self-hosted personal assistant where people hold conversations and ask the assistant to perform defined actions.

## Language

**User**:
A person with a local account whose conversations and settings are private to them.
_Avoid_: Account, profile

**Conversation**:
A persistent exchange of text messages between a user and the assistant.
_Avoid_: Chat, thread, session

**Skill**:
A named capability the assistant can invoke to perform an action or retrieve information beyond producing a conversational response.
_Avoid_: Plugin, tool, integration
